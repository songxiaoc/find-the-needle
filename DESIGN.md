## Overview

The template ships a safe tactical default. Replace this file when you change the visual contract.

## Colors

Use one game-specific accent over readable surfaces. Preserve contrast and do not place long text over noisy game imagery.

## Typography

Display type may carry the visual identity; body text remains readable and neutral. Monospace labels are reserved for compact metadata.

## Layout

Maintain a stable reading column, responsive navigation and content-first sections.

## Shapes

The safe default uses precise panels. The UI recipe may choose square, soft or rounded shapes.

## Components

Only template-supported heroes, navigation modes, cards and article layouts may be selected by the recipe.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do validate desktop and mobile output. Do not treat the template default as bespoke game art direction.
