## How this page exists

You added one `.mdx` file. fumadocs picked it up at build time, gave it a route
(`/guides/bosses/example-boss`), extracted this heading into the table of
contents, and listed it under the Bosses category in navigation. No `page.tsx`,
no registry entry.
