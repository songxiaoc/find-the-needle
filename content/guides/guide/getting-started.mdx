Start by clearing hay, selling what you collect and reinvesting in equipment. Finding needles is the goal; building a reliable way to move the hay makes the search more manageable.

This guide covers the Steam game by FindTheNeedleDev. If you have not installed it yet, start with the [demo and PC requirements](/guides/guide/demo).

## Make your first collecting trips useful

Before spending your earnings, try one complete collection-and-sale cycle. Notice which part takes the most effort: gathering the hay, moving it, or dealing with it after collection.

The game offers manual equipment including shovels, buckets, wheelbarrows, pitchforks and vacuums. Our opening recommendation is to buy for the job you are doing now. Compare the equipment available in your game before paying for a tool that duplicates something you already do comfortably.

## Move from tools to a working line

![A large haystack fills the warehouse, with the hay sale area beside the left wall.](/images/find-the-needle/screenshot-0.webp)

The official screenshot shows space around the pile. Keep a route open between your work area and the sale point as you build.

Machinery introduces another task: getting material into and through your setup. Belts, processing equipment and power connections work together. A machine purchase is more useful when you can also afford to connect and feed it.

For your first line, keep the layout easy to follow. Watch one section run before extending it. If material stops moving, tracing a short route is easier than troubleshooting several new branches at once.

Use the [automation guide](/guides/guide/automation) when you are ready to plan that first production line.

## Keep the search in view

The metal detector and later scanning equipment are part of the needle hunt. Check your current in-game prompts as you unlock equipment, and leave room to reach the pile while expanding your factory.

The official description leaves the ending a mystery. You can learn the collection and production loop without looking up ending spoilers.
