**A needle can travel inside a hay product.** The developer explains that a <EntityLink kind="machines" id="silo">Silo</EntityLink> packages needles into hay wads, and that needles sold with products at the <EntityLink kind="machines" id="hay-sell-stand">Hay Sell Stand</EntityLink> return to the haystack. Clearing the visible pile and successfully collecting its needles are therefore different things.

## Follow the products, not just the pile

In a September 13 developer reply, FindTheNeedleDev says that the Silo does not normally destroy a needle: it can leave inside a hay wad or another hay product. If that product goes straight to the sale point, the needle is sold and returned to the pile.

If you think a needle has passed through your factory:

1. Read the status of machines holding or packaging material, especially the Silo.
2. Follow the output from those machines toward the scanner and sale point.
3. Check each branch, including routes that rejoin after your scanner.
4. Watch a few products through the route to distinguish a working connection from a belt that merely passes nearby.

## Silo says a needle is waiting

In the same discussion, a player reported **44 Loose Straw, 1 Needle Waiting**, with no further Silo output. Another player suggested adding enough hay to produce another wad. That is a community suggestion, not a developer-confirmed fix, and the displayed amount is from that player's case.

Before experimenting, [back up your save](/guides/guide/demo). Do not dismantle the Silo as a guaranteed recovery method: another reply reported a needle-destruction warning when doing so. Stop if the game warns that your action will destroy something.

## A needle was sold despite having a scanner

In a September 21 discussion, players suggested checking whether robotic arms could place material onto another reachable belt when the main belt was congested. Observe your own arms and nearby lines before rebuilding anything. If a product takes a side route, verify whether that route passes through a scanner too.

For a queue in front of a scanner, the [automation guide](/guides/guide/automation) covers the community suggestion of parallel scanning lanes. There is no verified universal machine count or guaranteed processing rate here.

A recent routing example: in the public transcript of [Z1 Gaming's October 7 video, 50:55–54:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28srflEVcDc&t=3055s), the creator notices that new drone and other collection lines are not being scanned, adds scanners, then reports the routes covered. Our takeaway is to trace each new collection input through a scanner after expanding a factory, and check for queues before merging flows. This is transcript-based commentary, not a frame-verified layout, a universal scanner ratio or a confirmed missing-needle fix.

Manual searching can also overlap with automation. In the public Japanese transcript of [いぬけん's October 9, 2026 video, 17:04–18:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c83SVXqpgho&t=1024s), a needle being uncovered is carried away by a nearby arm; the player follows it along the belt and eventually retrieves it. Our precaution: use the available controls to pause nearby collectors before reaching for an exposed needle, then restore the line after securing it. This is advice drawn from one transcript, not a frame-verified recovery method. Do not copy the video's belt dismantling as a guaranteed fix or ignore destruction warnings.

## Stuck at 5/6 with no hay left?

Players have reported this exact state. As checked on **September 27, 2026**, the linked reports do not contain a developer-confirmed general fix. The normal return-to-pile mechanism does not establish that every missing-needle bug resolves itself.

Keep your save. Record the needle count, remaining hay, any Silo status and your route through the scanners, then add those details to a relevant Steam discussion. Avoid deleting progress or dismantling the whole factory just to test a guess.

Use the [line check](/tools/line-check) to trace a route through supply, machines and scanners before changing your factory.
